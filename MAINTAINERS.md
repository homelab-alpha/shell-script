# Maintainers

Our goal is to ensure the long-term viability and continuous improvement of this
project. To achieve this, we have established a clear and structured process for
adding new maintainers and defining their roles and responsibilities. We believe
in fostering a collaborative environment where diverse contributions are
recognized and valued.

## Becoming a Maintainer

> [!NOTE]
>
> At this time, we are not accepting new Maintainers or Junior Maintainers. See
> **[Current Maintainers](#current-maintainers)** for details.

If you are interested in contributing to the project's long-term success by
becoming a maintainer, you will need to demonstrate a history of sustained and
meaningful contributions. These contributions showcase your commitment and
understanding of the project's codebase and community guidelines.

Your contributions can include, but are not limited to:

- **Pull Request Reviews**: Actively reviewing existing pull requests, providing
  constructive feedback, suggesting improvements, and helping ensure code
  quality and adherence to project standards.
- **Code Contributions**: Submitting pull requests that introduce new features,
  resolve reported bugs, improve existing functionality, or enhance
  documentation.
- **Issue Management**: Participating in the discussion and resolution of
  reported issues, including reproducing bugs or validating fixes, providing
  support to other users, and helping categorize and prioritize tasks.
- **Community Engagement**: Actively participating in project discussions,
  offering guidance to new contributors, and fostering a positive and welcoming
  community environment.

While your contributions do not need to be extensive in scale, consistency and
quality are key. We value a steady commitment to the project over infrequent,
large contributions.

### Process to Become a Junior Maintainer

1. Accumulate a clear record of ongoing, valuable contributions to the project,
   aligning with the types of contributions outlined above.
2. Open a pull request to add your GitHub handle and a direct link to your
   GitHub profile under the [Current Maintainers](#current-maintainers) section
   in this document, indicating your aspiration to become a Junior Maintainer.
   Your pull request description must clearly outline your motivations and
   highlight your most significant contributions.
3. The existing maintainers will review your pull request, assess your past
   contributions, and hold an internal discussion regarding your nomination.
   They may reach out for further clarification.
4. Upon successful approval, your pull request will be merged and you will
   officially be granted the **Junior Maintainer** role, along with the
   necessary permissions and responsibilities.

## Maintainer Progression

The process above is the entry point for **Junior Maintainer**. Promotion to
**Maintainer** and subsequently to **Senior Maintainer** is handled differently.
These roles are granted based on merit, demonstrated expertise, consistent
contributions, and trust earned within the project.

Progression to a higher role is an internal process, initiated by existing
Maintainers or the Project Founder, and does not require a pull request from the
candidate. It is a recognition of sustained commitment and leadership.

- **Junior Maintainer → Maintainer**: Based on sustained contributions and
  demonstrated leadership. Existing maintainers discuss and nominate candidates;
  the Project Founder makes the final decision.
- **Maintainer → Senior Maintainer**: Reflects deep expertise, ongoing
  commitment, and the ability to mentor others. Initiated by existing Senior
  Maintainers or the Project Founder.

## Maintainer Roles and Responsibilities

### Project Founder

- **Role**: The initial creator and ultimate decision-maker of the project.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Defining the project's strategic direction and vision.
  - Making final decisions on major changes and features.
  - Appointing and dismissing other project roles.
  - Overseeing the overall health and sustainability of the project.

### Senior Maintainer

- **Role**: Highly experienced contributors with deep knowledge of the codebase
  and project goals. They hold primary responsibility for technical quality and
  coordination.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Performing in-depth code reviews and approving major pull requests.
  - Making technical architecture decisions.
  - Coordinating releases and version management.
  - Guiding and mentoring other maintainers and contributors.
  - Managing complex technical issues and disputes.
  - Actively contributing to the project roadmap.

### Maintainer

- **Role**: Experienced contributors responsible for specific parts of the
  project or regular day-to-day tasks.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Performing code reviews and merging pull requests.
  - Implementing bugfixes and new features according to project guidelines.
  - Maintaining and improving documentation.
  - Managing issues and discussions in the community.
  - Contributing to the project's testing and quality assurance.

### Junior Maintainer

- **Role**: Less experienced maintainers who work under the supervision of
  Maintainers or Senior Maintainers.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Performing code reviews and fixing bugs, often with guidance from more
    experienced maintainers.
  - Assisting with documentation and issue management.
  - Submitting minor pull requests and providing assistance to the community.
  - Actively learning and developing under mentorship.

## Additional Guidelines

### Decision-Making Process

Decisions concerning major changes, features, or maintainer appointments are
made through **consensus** among Senior Maintainers and the Project Founder. The
**Project Founder retains ultimate veto rights** on all project decisions to
ensure alignment with the project's long-term vision. Collaborative
decision-making is prioritized, but the project's core direction remains intact.

### Activity Expectations

To ensure the project remains active and well-maintained, all maintainers are
expected to show consistent engagement:

- **Junior Maintainers**: At least **one meaningful contribution** per month
  (e.g., pull request review, bugfix, documentation update).
- **Maintainers**: At least **two meaningful contributions** per month (e.g.,
  pull request review, feature implementation, issue management).
- **Senior Maintainers**: Regular in-depth code reviews, active participation in
  architectural discussions, and mentoring. No fixed numerical minimum, as
  contributions are often strategic and high in impact rather than high in
  volume.

A **meaningful contribution** is defined as an action that demonstrably adds
value to the project, such as resolving a bug, adding a feature, significantly
improving documentation, or providing a detailed and constructive pull request
review that leads to improvement.

If a maintainer is inactive for more than **three consecutive months** without
prior communication, the Project Founder and Senior Maintainers will initiate a
review that may lead to a discussion about their continued role or, if
necessary, their removal from the active maintainer list.

### Stepping Down or Removal

- **Voluntary Resignation**: Notify the Project Founder or a Senior Maintainer
  via a formal email. A formal announcement will then be made to the community.
- **Involuntary Removal**: In cases of prolonged inactivity or violations of
  community guidelines or the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), the
  Project Founder in consultation with Senior Maintainers may remove a
  maintainer. The decision is communicated privately first, followed by a
  community announcement if deemed necessary.

### Conflict Resolution

- **Open Discussion**: The first step is always an open and respectful
  discussion among the involved parties to find common ground.
- **Mediation by Senior Maintainer**: If no resolution is reached, a neutral
  Senior Maintainer (not directly involved in the conflict) will mediate.
- **Project Founder's Decision**: If mediation fails, the Project Founder makes
  the final binding decision.

### Communication Channels

Maintainers primarily communicate via:

- **Email**: For day-to-day discussions, quick updates, and informal
  problem-solving.
- **GitHub Issues and Pull Request Comments**: For discussions directly related
  to code, features, and bugs.

### Contribution Review Frequency

- **Timely Acknowledgment**: New pull requests will be acknowledged within **5
  business days**.
- **Active Review**: Complete review cycles for straightforward contributions
  are targeted within **two weeks**. Complex contributions may take longer, and
  any anticipated delays will be communicated clearly.

### CODEOWNERS File

Code ownership is defined in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](./.github/CODEOWNERS). This
file:

- Automatically requests reviews from the appropriate maintainers when a pull
  request modifies code in their area of responsibility.
- Clearly defines who is responsible for which parts of the codebase.
- Directs pull requests to the most knowledgeable reviewers to streamline the
  review process.

### Security Considerations

All maintainers are expected to be aware of and adhere to our
[Security Policy](./SECURITY.md). Reported security vulnerabilities are handled
by **Senior Maintainers** or the **Project Founder** in accordance with that
policy.

### Project Roadmap

The project's strategic direction is guided by the
[Project Roadmap](./ROADMAP.md). This living document outlines long-term goals,
major features, and key initiatives. Senior Maintainers are expected to actively
contribute to and help shape the roadmap.
